Danielle E. Gary

Director of Social Services Blooming College Academies

Danielle E. Gary is the Director of Social Services at Blooming College Academies. Danielle contributes more than a decade of experience providing direct services to youth.

Danielle's clinical work includes leading life skills groups, health education classes, crisis response, individual therapy, family therapy, and self-sufficiency planning. She uses evidence-based approaches to empower and educate youth. Her clinical approach helps youth process their emotions, cope with setbacks, and build resilience.

Danielle is an alumna of The City College of New York (CUNY) and John Jay College of Criminal Justice (CUNY), earning degrees in Psychology and Forensic Mental Health Counseling, respectively.

Christian Lee

Director of IT and Finance Blooming Academies Foundation

Christian Lee serves as Director of IT and Finance for Blooming Academies Foundation, where he built the organization's comprehensive multi-school financial model supporting its four-school charter network and leads its web and technology infrastructure.

He brings a background in education technology from his work at SchoolAI, where he helps K-12 districts bring tools into the classroom that save teachers time and allow them to reach every student individually. Our People · Governance

The board that keeps us rooted.

Our Board of Directors provides the governance, oversight, and strategic direction behind Blooming College Academies and the Blooming Academies Foundation. Directors serve voluntarily, bringing decades of industry, civic, and educational leadership to the students in our care.

Looking for the leaders who run our schools day to day?

How We Govern

Four commitments that guide every board decision.

1

Fiduciary Oversight

The Board safeguards the mission, approves the budget, and holds the organization accountable to families, funders, and the state.

2

Academic Accountability

Directors review academic outcomes against the SEED methodology so instructional decisions stay anchored to student results.

3

Community Representation

Directors bring professional and lived experience from the communities we serve, and they stay reachable to the families in them.

4

Long-Term Stewardship

The Board sets strategy for sustainable growth across the Blooming Academies Foundation school network.

Clayton W. Robinson, MSE, MBA, PHD CANDIDATE

Member, Board of Directors Vice President, Transmission & Grid Integration · Cordelio Power

A seasoned energy executive, electrical engineer, and strategic leader with more than 20 years of experience advancing the electric utility and renewable energy industries.

Clayton W. Robinson is a seasoned energy executive, electrical engineer, and strategic leader with more than 20 years of experience advancing the electric utility and renewable energy industries. As Vice President of Transmission & Grid Integration at Cordelio Power, he leads the transmission interconnection strategy for one of North America's growing renewable energy portfolios, overseeing the integration of utility-scale wind, solar, and battery energy storage projects into the electric grid.

Throughout his career, Clayton has built a reputation for delivering innovative, practical solutions to complex power system challenges. His expertise spans transmission planning, grid integration, renewable project development, power system engineering, financial valuation, mergers and acquisitions due diligence, regulatory compliance, and utility operations. He has managed multi-billion-dollar renewable development portfolios, negotiated Large Generator Interconnection Agreements representing more than 8 GW of renewable generation, and directed capital programs exceeding $450 million.

Before joining Cordelio Power, Clayton held leadership positions with NextEra Energy and Consolidated Edison Company of New York, where he played key roles in power system operations, engineering, emergency response, grid modernization, storm hardening initiatives, and the development of New York's first utility-integrated microgrid. His work has consistently focused on improving grid reliability, operational excellence, and the successful transition to a cleaner, more resilient energy future.

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Clayton is deeply committed to community service and leadership. He serves on the Board of Directors for the Friends of Porus Association Inc. (FOP), a New York-based nonprofit dedicated to supporting charitable, educational, and community development initiatives in Jamaica, and as Chairperson of the organization’s Student Affairs Committee, where he helps develop educational opportunities, mentorship programs, and initiatives that empower students and invest in the next generation of leaders.

Clayton also brings more than 10 years of entrepreneurial business operating experience, including retail and real estate holdings in his local community.

Driven by a passion for innovation, servant leadership, and sustainable development, Clayton believes that strong leadership, technical excellence, and community engagement are essential to creating lasting impact. His mission is to help build a more reliable, resilient, and sustainable energy future while contributing to the growth and development of communities at home and abroad.
